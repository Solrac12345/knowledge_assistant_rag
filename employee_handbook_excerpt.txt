EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK EXCERPT - 2026

SECTION 1: VACATION POLICY
All full-time employees are entitled to 15 working days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation days accrue monthly and may be carried over up to 5 days into the next year. Requests must be submitted via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. For questions, contact Johanna Verbom in HR.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests should be submitted every Monday via Slack to your direct supervisor. Equipment stipends of up to $500 are available for home office setup. IT support for remote employees is available Monday-Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM EST.

SECTION 3: HEALTH BENEFITS
The company provides comprehensive health insurance including medical, dental, and vision coverage. Employees can choose between PPO and HMO plans during open enrollment in November. Dependents may be added within 30 days of hire. For benefits questions, email benefits@company.com or call extension 222.

SECTION 4: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
Each employee receives an annual learning stipend of $1,200 for courses, conferences, or certifications. Requests must include a business justification and be approved by your manager. Reimbursement is processed within 2 weeks of course completion. Popular platforms include Coursera, Udemy, and LinkedIn Learning.

SECTION 5: EMERGENCY CONTACTS
In case of workplace emergency, dial 911 first, then notify security at extension 999. The emergency assembly point is the north parking lot. Emergency contacts for HR: Marcus Chen (x101), Sofia Rodriguez (x102). For medical emergencies, the nearest hospital is City General at 123 Health Ave.

SECTION 6: CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees must complete annual ethics training by March 31. Violations of the code of conduct should be reported anonymously via the Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199 or ethics@company.com. Retaliation against reporters is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.
